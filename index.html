Auction Baby — Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 21, 2026

Overview

Auction Baby is a dating entertainment app. This build is fully local: no account server, no analytics SDKs, no tracking. All profiles are fictional and all in-app currency is play money.

Data We Collect

  • None. The current build does not collect, transmit, or store any personal data. All app data is stored locally on your device.

Data Used to Track You

None. Auction Baby does not track you across apps or websites.

Data Linked to You

None. Auction Baby does not link any data to your identity.

Data Not Linked to You

  • Purchases: In-app purchases (IAP) are processed by Apple via StoreKit. Apple may link purchase history to your Apple ID. Auction Baby does not receive your Apple ID or personal information from purchases.

In-App Purchases

Auction Baby offers consumable currency (Gavels) and auto-renewable subscriptions (Auction Baby Pass) via Apple's StoreKit 2. Payments are processed by Apple. The app does not process real date payments — those are a real-world, person-to-person service that happens entirely outside the app.

AI-Generated Content

Some profiles in the app are AI-generated "Copycat" lures. These are clearly labeled with a "Copycat · AI" badge. No real user data is used to generate these profiles.

Children's Privacy

Auction Baby is rated 17+ and is not intended for children. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes to This Policy

If we update this policy, we will post the new version on this page.
